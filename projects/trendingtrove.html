Automated Video Production

Visual summary. The clip alternates product photos, close-ups and short text overlays. A final slide brings the four featured products together.

From script to render

The Python workflow passes product information to a local Qwen model, uses Kokoro for speech synthesis and assembles timed scenes and captions for FFmpeg. The project documentation also describes a PySide6 desktop interface.

What the audit verified

The November 2026 audit matched the clip byte for byte with the archived project output and inspected the product, script, audio, storyboard and rendering modules. A fresh full render and Windows runtime validation are still open.
